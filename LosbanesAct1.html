Paris: The City of Love

Paris, the capital and largest city of France, is renowned for its rich cultural heritage and breathtaking landmarks. From leisurely strolls along the Seine to world-class museums and exquisite French cuisine, the city offers visitors an unforgettable experience.

Yet what fascinates me most is its nickname: the "City of Love." How does a city earn a title so magical?


This vacation, I spent two unforgettable days in this beautiful, romantic city. I started by planning where to stay and which places to visit, making sure to leave room for trying local cuisine and sharing a dinner with my loved ones right beneath the Eiffel Tower. This adventure promised to be full of surprises and here's my two-day itinerary. Come along, and let me be your guide.

Day 1: Interacting with Locals and Exploring the City's Culture

On the first day of my trip to Paris, I decided to explore the city by bike, because it let me slow down in ways a taxi or the metro never could. Weaving through different arrondissements at my own pace. Pausing at corner cafés and museums and striking up small conversations with locals, I realised that exploring Paris wasn't just about seeing landmarks but it was about feeling the city through its people.

8:00 AM - History and perspective at Canal Saint-Martin

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

I started my day by biking along Canal Saint-Martin, striking up conversations with locals about their daily lives and asking them why they think their city is called the City of Love. It was a wonderful way to begin the day.

9:30 AM - Breakfast at Café Méricourt

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

After talking with the locals, I stopped for breakfast at Café Méricourt, enjoying fresh pastries and coffee. The pastries were flaky and still warm.

11:00 AM - Art and artifacts at the Louvre Museum

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

I lost myself in the galleries of the Louvre. Two paintings gave me two very different perspectives on the meaning of love. The Death of Sardanapalus by Eugène Delacroix portrays love as a destructive force. One that seems to say, "If I can't have it, no one can." Psyche Revived by Cupid's Kiss by Antonio Canova, on the other hand, portrays love as a transformative force, capable of bringing life and hope even to the most hopeless situations. Standing between these two works, I realized love isn't just a feeling but it is a powerful force that shapes our lives in ways we never imagined.

2:00 PM - Lunch at Loulou

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

After a morning of exploring, I recharged with lunch at Loulou, a chic restaurant in the heart of Paris. The food was exquisite and the ambiance perfect for a relaxing meal. I tried their signature dish, a delightful combination of flavors that left me wanting more.

3:30 PM - Musée des Arts et Métiers with family

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

I returned my bike and booked a ride to Musée des Arts et Métiers, where I spent the rest of the afternoon exploring exhibits on the history of science and technology. The museum's collection of inventions was truly amazing, and I loved sharing the experience with my family.

5:30 PM - Street food and street life

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Walking the streets afterward, we stopped at different vendors to try their food from savory crêpes to sweet pastries. Each bite was a delightful experience of Parisian culture, and with a of mix vibrant street life and aroma of fresh food made the evening truly memorable.

7:00 PM - Family dinner at Molitor Hotel & Spa Paris

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

We returned to our hotel and closed out the day with a family dinner at its restaurant. Over a delicious meal, we shared stories and laughter, reflecting on everything we'd experienced. Afterward, we relaxed by the hotel's swimming pool before heading to bed, grateful for the first day in Paris.

Day 2: The Heart of Paris

On our second day, we turned our attention to the historic and spiritual heart of the city, from a quiet morning of reflection to an evening of romance along the Seine and Eiffel Tower.

9:30 AM - Notre-Dame Cathedral

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

After breakfast at the hotel, our family visited Notre-Dame Cathedral to give thanks for the blessings that had made our lives so full of adventure. Afterward, we took photos around the cathedral to remember the moment.

10:30 AM - Île de la Cité & Square du Vert-Galant

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

From the cathedral, we made our way to Île de la Cité, the historic birthplace of Paris, and rest at Square du Vert-Galant, a quiet, tree-lined park at the very tip of the island, right where the Seine splits in two. I sketched the point where the river separates, and it struck me that, much like a river, a family sometimes separates for the betterment of its members and yet they still remains a family.

11:00 AM - An unexpected stop at Père Lachaise Cemetery

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

I recalled a local's story about Père Lachaise Cemetery that somewhere within it may lay an answer to my question of why Paris is called the City of Love. I find it unlikely because a cemetery often carries only grief. But I discovered the cemetery holds one of history's most enduring love stories: Abelard and Héloïse, a medieval lovers whose letters still resonate with passion and heartbreak, are buried together there. I shared their story with my family and left flowers at their grave before we left.

1:00 PM - Lunch at Il Était Un Square

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

We are craving something delicious after our morning activities, so we tracked down what's said to be one of the best burger spots in Paris and it didn't disappoint. We savored every bite, and I also took photos so I could try recreating their plating at home.

3:00 PM - Locks at Pont des Arts

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

After lunch, we made our way to Pont des Arts, the famous bridge where couples attach locks as a symbol of eternal love. Our family chose one large lock to symbolize that we are bound together. They were taking photos while I read what other visitors had written on theirs.

4:00 PM - Sunset cruise on the Seine

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

We booked a cruise to watch the sunset over the Seine, the crew serve us ice cream and drinks. I even attempted to paint the sunset myself. The result was better than average.

7:00 PM - Dinner at Le Jules Verne

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

To end the trip, I surprised my family with a reservation at Le Jules Verne, a two-Michelin-starred restaurant. A place I'd always dreamed of visiting. Watching people savoring quiet moments together and a breathtaking night view of the city, we ate through the full tasting menu, comparing each dishes to the food back home.

Growing up, I came to understand love in different forms starting from my favorite stuffed toys, my hobbies, and eventually the people I care about. So when I return to that original question, how can a city be called something so magical? I think my time in Paris is the answer. This is a city where love shows up everywhere: in the warmth of locals, in candlelit dinners shared with someone special, in the care chefs pour into a dish not just to serve it but to honor their craft, and in the quiet act of wandering streets that have carried romance for centuries.
